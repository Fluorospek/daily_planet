NEWSROOM NOTE: Advance reporting draft, shared with staff reporters ahead of publication for awareness and follow-up assignment planning. Not yet released to the public.

Council members are expected to vote next Tuesday on the transit funding package after months of debate, according to three council staffers who described the state of negotiations on condition they not be named ahead of the public session.

The current draft would raise the parking surcharge introduced with the arena budget by an additional quarter point to fund bus and rail improvements, rather than create a separate transit-specific tax as originally proposed.

Two council members who previously opposed any new transit funding are now reportedly open to the compromise, which could give the measure the votes it needs to pass. A final text of the ordinance has not been made public.

Reporters should expect the administration to formally unveil the compromise language at Monday's public briefing, one day before the scheduled vote.
