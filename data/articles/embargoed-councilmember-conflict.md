EDITOR'S NOTE: This is an unpublished investigative draft. Do not cite or reference until legal review is complete and the story clears fact-checking. Visibility restricted to editorial staff only.

Public records reviewed by this reporter show that Diana Prince, who chairs the city council's finance committee, held an undisclosed minority stake in a subsidiary of Wayne Construction Partners, the lead contractor awarded the arena's structural work, at the time the council voted on the arena budget.

The stake, held through a family trust, was not listed on Prince's most recent public financial disclosure filing. City ethics rules require council members to disclose any financial interest exceeding five thousand dollars in a firm doing business with the city.

This reporter has not yet obtained comment from Prince or her office. The story is being held pending a response and independent verification of the trust filing through the county clerk's office.

If confirmed, the omission could trigger an ethics review and calls for Prince to recuse herself from future votes involving Wayne Construction Partners.
