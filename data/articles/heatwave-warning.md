---
title: Heat advisory issued as temperatures set to climb this weekend
date: 2026-08-10
author: Daily Planet Staff
section: Weather
publication: Daily Planet
---
The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the metro area, with temperatures expected to reach 39 degrees Celsius over the weekend and little relief overnight.

Officials are urging residents to stay hydrated, avoid strenuous activity during the afternoon, and check on elderly neighbors and those without air conditioning. The heat is expected to peak on Saturday.

The city is opening six cooling centers in libraries and community halls, which will operate from 9 in the morning until 8 in the evening through Sunday. A full list of locations is available on the city's website.

Emergency officials warned that parked cars can become dangerous within minutes and reminded residents never to leave children or pets inside a vehicle. Outdoor workers are advised to take frequent breaks in the shade.

Cooler air is expected to move in early next week, bringing temperatures back to seasonal levels.
