---
title: Transit funding debate heats up ahead of September vote
date: 2026-07-28
author: Iris West
section: City
publication: Daily Planet
---
With a vote on transit improvements scheduled for September, city council members clashed this week over how to fund an aging bus and rail network that carries more than 300,000 riders a day.

At the heart of the debate is a proposed 90 million dollar package to add bus routes, extend service hours, and begin repairs on the East Line, which has suffered repeated delays. Supporters say the investment is overdue.

"People are waiting forty minutes for a bus that used to come every ten," said council member Iris West, no relation to this reporter, who has championed the package. "Reliable transit is not a luxury. For many residents it is the difference between keeping a job and losing one."

Opponents question the timing, coming so soon after the city committed hundreds of millions to the downtown arena. They argue the city cannot take on both without raising taxes or cutting other services.

Transit advocates point out that the arena and transit budgets draw from different funds, and that improved service around the riverfront will be essential once the arena opens and draws large crowds.

A public hearing on the transit package is scheduled for the last week of August, before the council's September vote.
