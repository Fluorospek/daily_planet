Residents react to arena vote

Hours after the city council approved the downtown arena budget, residents gathered at the riverfront to share their views, and the mood was decidedly mixed.

"I have lived here my whole life and this district has been empty for twenty years," said Rosa Delgado, a teacher. "If this brings it back to life, I am all for it."

Others were less certain. "Four hundred and eighty million dollars is a staggering number," said James Olsen, a small-business owner. "I just hope the promises about jobs and safeguards actually hold up once the cameras leave."

Community groups say they will watch the quarterly audits closely and hold the city to its commitments on local hiring and support for affected businesses.
